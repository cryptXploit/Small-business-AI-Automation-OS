## 3. FIELD DICTIONARY & AUTOMATION PROPERTIES

Your starter database comes pre-configured with operational fields:

- **`Lead_ID`**: Unique tracking identifier (`LD-2026-001`, etc.).
- **`Contact_Name` & `Business_Or_Household`**: Primary prospect details.
- **`Industry_Segment`**: Target market vertical for automated routing.
- **`Inbound_Channel`**: Web form, Google Ads, Phone, Referral, or Direct Chat.
- **`Lead_Received_Timestamp` & `First_Touch_Timestamp`**: Clock precision for response time.
- **`Speed_To_Lead_Seconds`**: The exact elapsed seconds between receipt and human or AI contact.
- **`SLA_60s_Status`**: `PASS (<60s)` or `BREACH (>60s)`.
- **`Urgency_Tier`**: Priority ranking (`Tier 1 Immediate`, `Tier 2 High`, `Tier 3 Standard`).
- **`Qualification_Score`**: 1–100 automated scoring index.
- **`Recommended_Quote_Tier`**: Matched offer tier (`Tier 1`, `Tier 2 Recommended`, `Tier 3 VIP`).
- **`Pipeline_Stage`**: Pipeline state (`New Inbound`, `Qualified`, `Quote Sent`, `Closed Won`).
- **`Workflow_Blueprint_Ref`**: Direct link to your Make/n8n automation scenario.
