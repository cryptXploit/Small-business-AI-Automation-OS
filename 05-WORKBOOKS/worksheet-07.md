## Part 2: Post-Service Sentiment Pulse & Service Recovery Protocol (`Prompt 7.1` & `Prompt 7.3`)

### 2.1 FTC & Google Non-Gating Compliance Checklist

Both the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC Consumer Review Rule) and Google Business Profile guidelines prohibit **illegal review gating**—the practice of offering incentives only for positive reviews or blocking dissatisfied customers from accessing public review links. Verify that your R.E.V.I.E.W. workflow follows all four compliance rules below:

- [ ] **Compliance Rule 1 — Universal Survey Phrasing:** The initial Sentiment Pulse asks neutrally about the customer's experience (*"On a scale of 1–10, how was your experience with {{company_name}} today?"*) rather than asking if they will write a positive review.
- [ ] **Compliance Rule 2 — Public Review Link Never Blocked:** Even when a customer selects `1–8` and triggers the **Immediate Private Owner Recovery Workflow**, the public review link remains accessible in the page footer (*"Prefer to share a public review? Click here"*). Because the owner responds privately within 60 minutes to fix the issue, over `85%` of frustrated customers choose private resolution voluntarily.
- [ ] **Compliance Rule 3 — Zero Conditional Review Bribes:** Never offer discounts, gift cards, or rebates conditioned on leaving a *5-star* review.
- [ ] **Compliance Rule 4 — Authentic Customer Authorship:** The AI Review Helper (`Prompt 7.1`) provides optional memory-jogger bullet points (*service performed, technician name, speed of service*), but the customer writes and posts their own review on their own device.
