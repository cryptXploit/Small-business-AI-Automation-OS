## Part 3: Complete 90-Day Phased Automation Sprint Tracker (`Prompt 10.2`)

Never try to build all 10 chapters simultaneously on a single Tuesday. Follow this battle-tested **30 / 60 / 90-Day Phased Rollout Roadmap** (spending just **3 to 4 hours per week**) so every workflow stabilizes and generates cash before you layer on the next module.

### Phase I: Days 1–30 — Revenue Defense, Safety Gates & Speed-to-Lead (`Chapters 1, 2, 3, 4`)

* **Primary Goal:** Stop inbound lead leakage, enforce human-in-the-loop safety, and lift quote-to-close conversion by 25%–40%.
- [ ] **Week 1 (Audit & Architecture — `Chapters 1 & 2`):** Complete the 5-Day Time & Lead-Leak Audit (`Worksheet 1`), calculate your CMF, score workflows on the `A.U.T.O.` Matrix, and configure your Green/Amber/Red `T.R.A.V.` Risk Gates and `#amber-approvals` Slack/Teams channel (`Worksheet 2`).
- [ ] **Week 2 (60-Second Multi-Channel Lead Router — `Chapter 3`):** Deploy the `I.N.S.T.A.N.T.` Lead Router across web forms, Google LSA, and social DMs (`Prompts 3.2 & 3.3`), delivering sub-60-second responses and instant calendar booking links (`Worksheet 3`).
- [ ] **Week 3 (Missed-Call SMS Concierge & Voice-to-CRM — `Chapter 3`):** Activate instant missed-call text-back (`Prompt 3.1`) with A2P 10DLC compliance and connect voice-note discovery extraction (`Prompt 3.5`) into your CRM.
- [ ] **Week 4 (3-Option Instant Quoting & 14-Day Follow-Up — `Chapter 4`):** Standardize your Good-Better-Best (`Core / Pro / Complete`) pricebook (`Prompt 4.1`), activate the gross-margin validator (`Prompt 4.2`), and turn on the 5-touch 14-day quote nurture sequence with instant stop-on-reply (`Prompt 4.3` & `Worksheet 4`).
* **Day 30 Milestone Sign-Off:** Speed-to-Lead `< 60s` verified | Quote follow-up running | **Estimated Weekly Time Saved: `6–8 Hours/Week`**.

### Phase II: Days 31–60 — Frictionless Fulfillment, 24/7 Support & Cashflow (`Chapters 5, 6, 7, 8`)

* **Primary Goal:** Eliminate onboarding rework, automate 65%+ of support volume, multiply 5-star Google reviews, and cut DSO below 15 days.
- [ ] **Week 5 (Zero-Drop Customer Onboarding Protocol — `Chapter 5`):** Connect Stripe/Square/QBO deposit webhooks to auto-create client folders, send the 15-second Welcome Packet (`Prompt 5.2`), enforce Gate A asset completeness chasers (`Prompt 5.3`), and compile the 1-Page Internal Delivery Brief (`Prompt 5.1` & `Worksheet 5`).
- [ ] **Week 6 (The 3-Tier AI Customer Support Shield — `Chapter 6`):** Compile your 25-chunk Single-Source Knowledge Base (`Prompt 6.1`), connect read-only order/booking lookups, and activate Tier 1 autonomous replies (`Prompt 6.3`), Tier 2 context whispers (`Prompt 6.4`), and Tier 3 Red-Zone bot suppression (`Prompt 6.5` & `Worksheet 6`).
- [ ] **Week 7 (The R.E.V.I.E.W. & 90-Day Win-Back Flywheel — `Chapter 7`):** Launch the post-service 1-click Sentiment Pulse (`Prompt 7.1`), `< 60-minute` private service recovery (`Prompt 7.3`), 2-hour SEO review responder (`Prompt 7.2`), and 60/90/180-day dormant customer recall sequence (`Prompt 7.4` & `Worksheet 7`).
- [ ] **Week 8 (Milestone Invoicing & 5-Stage AR Chasers — `Chapter 8`):** Turn on instant milestone invoice drafting (`Prompt 8.1`) and the 5-stage polite-to-firm overdue payment chaser sequence (`Prompt 8.2`) with instant webhook stop-logic (`Worksheet 8`).
* **Day 60 Milestone Sign-Off:** Tier 1 Support `>= 60%` auto-resolved | DSO `< 18 days` | **Cumulative Weekly Time Saved: `15–20 Hours/Week`**.

### Phase III: Days 61–90 — Back-Office Reconciliation, Living SOPs & Owner Command (`Chapters 8, 9, 10`)

* **Primary Goal:** Automate receipt/vendor triage, extract the operations manual from the owner's head, and lock in the 15-Minute Friday Scorecard.
- [ ] **Week 9 (OCR Receipt Triage & 5-Day Monthly Close — `Chapter 8`):** Launch `receipts@yourcompany.com` with `Prompt 8.3` GL categorization, `Prompt 8.4` vendor price-creep (`> 5%`) alerts, and `Prompt 8.5` monthly pre-close missing-receipt chasers.
- [ ] **Week 10 (5-Minute Voice-to-SOP Vault & Delegation — `Chapter 9`):** Record your top 15 recurring tasks on Loom/voice, compile them into 7-Block Living SOPs (`Prompt 9.1`), and enforce the 5-point Voice-to-Checklist delegation standard (`Prompt 9.2` & `Worksheet 9`).
- [ ] **Week 11 (Internal "Ask-the-SOP" Bot & 7-Day New-Hire Certification — `Chapter 9`):** Deploy your grounded internal `#ask-the-sop` staff assistant (`Prompt 9.4`), connect daily huddle action-item synthesis (`Prompt 9.3`), and build your 7-day role certification quizzes (`Prompt 9.5`).
- [ ] **Week 12 (The 15-Minute Friday Owner Scorecard & Quarterly Audit — `Chapter 10`):** Automate the Friday 3:00 PM 12-Metric Executive Brief (`Prompt 10.1`), Monday 15-Minute Huddle Priorities (`Prompt 10.5`), and Monthly/Quarterly ROI & Governance Audits (`Prompts 10.3 & 10.4`).
* **Day 90 Milestone Sign-Off:** All 5 Department Engines live | Owner working `<= 35 hrs/wk` | **Cumulative Weekly Time Saved: `22–32 Hours/Week`**.
