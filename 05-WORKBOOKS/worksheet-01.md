## Executive Instructions: How to Run This Audit Without Slowing Down Your Week

Most small business owners underestimate their repetitive administrative load by 40% to 60% because manual routing happens in two-minute increments throughout the day—answering a quick staff text, copying an email address from a web form into a phone contact, looking up whether an invoice was paid, or typing the same four paragraphs into a customer estimate. Those micro-interruptions fragment deep work and quietly consume 15 to 25 hours every week.

To capture an accurate operational baseline without adding friction to your schedule, follow three rules for the next five business days:

1. **Log in Real Time, Not from Memory on Friday:** Keep this worksheet open on your desk (printed in US Letter/A4 or open inside the Tablet Planner / Excel Command Center). Whenever you or a key team member performs a repetitive task, switches between two software apps to copy-paste data, or answers a recurring question, log it in **Part A** immediately.
2. **Tag Every Entry to One of the 5 Core Departments:**
   * `Dept 01`: Lead Capture & Speed-to-Lead Intake
   * `Dept 02`: Sales Qualification, Estimating & Follow-Up
   * `Dept 03`: Customer Onboarding, Fulfillment & Support Triage
   * `Dept 04`: Invoicing, Receivables & Back-Office Administration
   * `Dept 05`: Team SOPs, Delegation & Weekly Executive Reporting
3. **Measure Actual Clock Time on Inbound Leads:** In **Part B**, pull the last 20 inbound inquiries across your phone log, website contact form, email inbox, SMS/WhatsApp, and social media messages. Calculate the exact number of minutes or hours between the timestamp of the customer's inquiry and your company's first meaningful human or automated response.
